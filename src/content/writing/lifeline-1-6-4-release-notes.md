We're excited to announce the latest Lifeline update (1.6.4) is [now available in the macOS App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id1526186940?pt=118017664&ct=saent-blog&mt=8). This version brings significant improvements to the auto-launch feature.

### **🚀 Instant auto-launch sessions**

Auto-launch now instantly starts a session after detecting keyboard or mouse activity instead of bringing up the session launch panel. Turn it on in Lifeline’s _Preferences_ under _Automation_.

### **🕓 A 5-second grace to cancel auto-launched sessions**

![](/images/saent/lifeline-1-6-4-release-notes/64795105caa1097a123d5853_auto-launch-Free.webp)

Easily cancel an auto-launched session, just in case you're not ready to start. Click **Cancel Session** before the button turns completely grey, then select “Cancel without snooze.”

### **💤 Simplified auto-launch snoozing**

You can now quickly disable auto-launch for five minutes, 15 minutes, one hour, or until tomorrow, allowing you to relax, surf the web, or chat with friends without interruption. Simply select your desired period from the _Cancel Session_ button’s dropdown menu.

![](/images/saent/lifeline-1-6-4-release-notes/6479517a0425a3ea119249e8_auto-launch-snooze-options-TINY.webp)

Remember, auto-launch doesn't override manual control of Lifeline; you can still start and stop sessions whenever you like.

### ⚡️ Track _everything_ automatically with zero-minute auto-launch

Want to track every minute you spend at your computer? Go to _Preferences_ and set auto-launch to zero minutes. Lifeline will then kick off sessions as soon as you do anything on your device.
