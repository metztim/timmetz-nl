# Deep Work

_"Professional activities performed in a state of distraction-free concentration that push your cognitive capabilities to their limit. These efforts create new value, improve your skill, and are hard to replicate."_ 

> Cal Newport, in his book, [Deep Work](https://medium.com/just-finished/deep-work-33db8bf14cdd)

**At the start of every workday during this Challenge, you aim to hit 90 minutes of uninterrupted concentration on one task or project.**

If you're new to deep work, this is not easy. In this case, you try to get as close to the goal as you can every day. Your objective will be to hit the 90-minute milestone at the end of the program.

**There's no failure, only not trying.**

Your coach and peers will be there along on the journey with you. They'll answer your questions and share their experiences and tips.
