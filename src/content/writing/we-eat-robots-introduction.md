**Welcome to** ***We Eat Robots*****, a publication dedicated to helping you harness the power of AI to transform your work and life.** As AI continues to revolutionize how we live and work, it's essential to stay ahead of the algorithms and learn how to leverage these tools to become more productive, creative, and effective.

I'm Tim Metz, an entrepreneur, content marketer, and productivity enthusiast passionate about AI's potential. Having co-founded Saent, the company behind the world's first focus device, and Lifeline, [an advanced Pomodoro timer for macOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id1526186940?pt=118017664&ct=WER&mt=8), I've explored the intersection of technology and productivity for years.

In my current role as the Marketing and AI Innovation lead at [Animalz](https://www.animalz.co/), a premier content marketing agency, I've witnessed firsthand the impact of AI on the creative industry. While the rapid advancements in AI can be exciting and intimidating, I believe that by embracing these tools and learning to work alongside them, we can unlock our full potential and become better thinkers, creators, and communicators.

**That's why I started** ***We Eat Robots*** **– to guide you through this uncharted territory and help you become a productive superhero in the age of AI.**

Our mission is simple: if you don't learn to eat robots for breakfast, you might end up as their lunch!
