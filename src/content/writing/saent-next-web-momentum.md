We have some exciting news: almost the entire core Saent team will be descending on New York in a couple of weeks for [The Next Web: Momentum](http://thenextweb.com/nyc/) conference! Because we’re such a widely distributed team, this will actually be the first time some of us meet in person. In total, we are traveling over 18,207 miles, from Beijing, Auckland, Los Angeles, Brooklyn, and Providence, to meet up!

‍**Even more exciting:** we were chosen to participate in the TNW SCALE at Momentum startup showcase. A small number startups were selected to present in the demo pit at the conference, so we’re extremely honored to be among them. We’re told the competition was fierce. Of those startups, _30 will get an opportunity to pitch on stage at Momentum_! We don’t have to tell you how awesome that would be for Saent. We want to spread the Saent story far and wide and we could use your help! From Nov. 1-4, anyone can vote on their favorite startups at SCALE. The top 20 will automatically be given a presentation slot. Click the button below to be taken to the [voting page](http://thenextweb.com/scale/vote/getsaent).

[Vote for Saent at TNW SCALE](http://thenextweb.com/scale/vote/getsaent)

Thanks! And we’d love to see you in New York. We’ll have a table in the demo pit, so come say hello._\-Josh, Maggie, Tim, and Russell_
