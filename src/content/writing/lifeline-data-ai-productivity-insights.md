I’ve spent the past weeks putting together a collection of queries for your data. You'll find them organized in [this Notion database](https://www.notion.so/72df210acf0c4c00a78f070231262afe?pvs=21). It's publicly viewable and, with a Notion account, you can leave comments and even add your own queries!

Here's how it works:

1.  Export your Lifeline data to CSV from the _Review stats and activities…_ menu.
2.  Attach the CSV to a chat in ChatGPT or Claude.
3.  Use a question from the database to analyze your data.

Make sure to check out [the video above for more details](https://www.loom.com/share/12ea05a6895e4d00b49011e2f2e8145e?sid=6c9db975-dfe9-4e09-920a-a2900652c184). And the more you track and label your sessions with Lifeline, the more interesting insights you’ll get from this approach.

## Three examples to get you inspired and started

Here are three examples of instructions you can give and the output they generate.

**_“What is the total number of Pomodoros completed on a week-by-week basis?”_**

This request for ChatGPT gives you a simple bar chart that shows how many pomodoros you’ve completed for every week in the period covered by the data set.

‍**_“Create a heatmap displaying the number of Pomodoros completed by the day of the week and the hour of the day on a cumulative basis for the entire period.”_**

Put this into ChatGPT, and you get a handy visual representation that shows on which days and hours you earned the most pomodoros.

![\_\_wf\_reserved\_inherit](/images/saent/lifeline-data-ai-productivity-insights/66f4bf56c905e076620099dc_66f4bf2dec5645482c8dc075_pomodoro-20heatmap.webp)

‍**_“What is the impact of the first work session's start time on the day's productivity?”_**

Here’s an excerpt of the response Claude gives when asked this question:

> "The most productive days often start between 7:30 AM and 8:30 AM. Many of the days with 12+ completed sessions began in this time range.  
>   
> Very early starts (before 7 AM) don't necessarily lead to more productivity. These days often have long breaks after the initial session, suggesting the early start might not be sustainable.  
>   
> Consistency seems to matter. Weeks with consistent early starts (like the first week of July) tend to be more productive overall than weeks with variable start times."

## Tips to get better results from your analysis

A few things to keep in mind when you analyze your Lifeline data with AI:

-   ChatGPT is great for creating diagrams and hard number crunching.
-   Claude’s 3.5 Sonnet model performs better than ChatGPT as a coach and analyst.
-   Claude is less precise and sometimes makes mistakes, so you need to stay on your toes.
-   Attach less data if you want to zoom in on a specific period. You can also export your data without breaks and untimed activity, reducing the size of each file.
-   Providing extra context at the start of a conversation is usually helpful. For example, that you were on vacation a certain week or how you use emoji labels.
