In the video below, agile coach [Henrik Kniberg](https://www.scrumalliance.org/community/profile/hkniberg) shows what happens when you work on many things simultaneously versus just one. What he demonstrates applies at the individual, team, and organizational levels.

I've never seen a more convincing and straightforward case for single-tasking. I'd love to [hear from you](https://twitter.com/timmetz) and will buy you a coffee if you still believe running many projects at once is effective after watching this video. 😄
