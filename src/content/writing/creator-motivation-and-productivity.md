I often write about boosting _your_ productivity.

What if you could turbocharge _someone else's_ productivity in less than five minutes?

Would you do it?

Because you can.

Let’s do it together right now:

1.  Think of a product, service, or content you enjoy, preferably from a small team or individual.

2.  Find their contact details on their website.

3.  Write a short message to express how much you enjoy what they make and how it makes your life better. Two paragraphs are enough; more is appreciated.

4.  Offer to let them use your message as a public testimonial (bonus).

5.  Hit _Send_.

Congratulations. 🥳

You've just made a creator's day and given them the motivation to keep pouring their love and creativity into their work.

## The power of positive feedback

Two things happened on the same day that inspired this article.

First, Gareth Davies, Content Lead at [Parabol](https://parabol.co/), shared [this post and image on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/garethadavies_when-someone-gives-parabol-a-g2-review-i-activity-7040067657311612929-Ek_H):

> When someone gives Parabol a G2 review, I can confirm it means so much! It gets routed through a public channel so we can all see it. It helps us build credibility externally and it boosts morale internally.If there’s a tool out there you love, and it’s built by a small team, a 5 minute review can go a really long way! And it doesn’t cost you a penny.
