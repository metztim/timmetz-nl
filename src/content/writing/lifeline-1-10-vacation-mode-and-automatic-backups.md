Lifeline 1.10 is live in [the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/saent-lifeline/id1492352560). This release is about stepping away from work without losing anything along the way: a proper vacation mode for when you are off the clock, and automatic backups that keep your history safe.

## Take real time off with Vacation mode

You can set a stop time for the end of a workday, but what about the days you are not working at all? Until now, Lifeline kept tracking right through your holidays, and coming back to a week of half-logged activity was a strange way to end a trip.

Vacation mode is here to fix that. Tell Lifeline the dates you are away, and while you are off it greets you with a calm full-screen rest screen instead of tracking your day. It shows which day of your vacation you are on, with a quote to mark the moment, and when you return it offers a gentle look back at the time you took off.

It is a greeting rather than a hard lock. If you do need to get to your computer, one click on "Use my computer" takes you straight through. The idea is a small reminder that you chose to step away, not a barrier that fights you.

You will find Vacation mode under _Preferences > Rest_, alongside Sign Off and your break settings.

## Your history, backed up automatically

Your activity history is one of the most useful things Lifeline gives you, a real record of how your days actually go. But it has always lived in a single place on your Mac, and one disk problem or a clumsy reinstall could wipe it out.

1.10 adds automatic backups. Point Lifeline at any folder, choose daily or weekly, and it quietly saves a copy of your activity data on that schedule, keeping the last fourteen so you can roll back if you need to. There is a "Back Up Now" button for an immediate copy, and restoring uses the same _File > Import_ you already know.

You will find the backup settings under _Preferences > Account_.

## Faster session naming

If you name your sessions, you probably reuse the same handful of labels week after week. As you type, Lifeline now shows a live list of your most-used labels, ranked across your whole history rather than just the last month, so the name you want is usually one keystroke and a tap away.

## A cleaner Preferences window

Preferences had grown to eleven sections in the sidebar, and finding the right one had become a hunt. This release rebuilds the window and groups related settings together, bringing the sidebar down to eight. Everything is still there, just easier to find.

## Smaller fixes

A few more improvements in this release:

-   **Phone notifications wait a beat.** The break-end notification to your phone now holds for thirty seconds, so sitting back down at your desk dismisses it before your pocket buzzes.
-   **Sign Off respects meetings that run long.** If a Lifeline meeting runs past your stop time, the post-meeting grace period now applies correctly, even after a restart.
-   **Steadier purchases, calendars, and editing.** This release fixes a stuck Buy Pro button, several calendar threading issues, and a handful of crashes around clearing history and editing activities.
