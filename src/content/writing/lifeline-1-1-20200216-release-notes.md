A new version of [Lifeline](https://www.saent.com/2020/01/11/what-lifeline-can-do-for-you/) is out. Here are the main updates:

-   Keyboard shortcuts are here; check them out in Preferences.
-   You can now disable the auto-timer popup, handy when you're not using your computer for work. Try it out in the taskbar menu.
-   The reminder popups received a visual make-over.
-   Speaking of reminder popups: try press and holding the snooze button to pick a snooze length.
-   Other small improvements, and of course...
-   Bug fixes

The update should arrive automatically but you can also update manually by choosing "Check for Updates..." from the taskbar dropdown menu.

_Don't have Lifeline yet? Head over to the macOS App Store and_ [_give it a try right now_](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id1526186940?pt=118017664&ct=saent-blog&mt=8)_._
