We're excited to announce [Lifeline version 1.6 is now available for download](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id1526186940?pt=118017664&ct=saent-blog&mt=8) from the macOS App Store! This update brings a fresh new look to Lifeline’s core experience. 🎉

### New and improved interface elements 🖥️

Now, you can enjoy a sleek, modern design that's visually appealing _and_ easier to navigate—no more rusty panels and confusing buttons.

We've eliminated several annoying and confusing pop-ups and cleaned up the taskbar menu.

![](/images/saent/lifeline-1-6-release/6430e0c052a859b6365737b1_04-Calendar-Integration.webp)

### What’s next?

You may still find some parts of the user experience (UX) that require a fresh look or update, one of the most important ones being the session auto-launcher.

Currently, when you have the auto-launch feature enabled, Lifeline displays the session launch panel when you’ve been working for a while without starting a session.

![](/images/saent/lifeline-1-6-release/6430e111d5e91942e33f1328_Lifeline-activity-detected.webp)

We’ll improve this experience in the next update. You’ll get a short notification similar to the one above. After this gentle reminder, Lifeline will open a launch panel with a snooze function.

![](/images/saent/lifeline-1-6-release/6430e14298764df25dd6a608_Lifeline-auto-launch-snooze.webp)

We hope you'll like this new UX as much as we do. We're constantly improving Lifeline, and your feedback is helpful to us. If you have questions, ideas, or suggestions, please [let us know](https://twitter.com/getsaent). We'd love to hear from you!
