After reading the excellent “[Etymology of EDM: The Complex Heritage Of Electronic Dance Music](https://medium.com/cuepoint/etymology-of-edm-the-complex-heritage-of-electronic-dance-music-d3e3aa873369)” by [Joshua Glazer](https://medium.com/@joshuaglazer), I had to think of a cartoon series we created at [DanceTrippin](http://www.dancetrippin.tv).

During the summer of 2012, we produced a new cartoon each week, to highlight the trending “hot news” in the dance scene at that moment. Reading Joshua’s article, I realised quite a few are still relevant, including one that was created precisely because of the “Mark Farrina VIP booth incident” he’s referring to:
