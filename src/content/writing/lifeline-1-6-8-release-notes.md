## 🍅 Get tomatoes for manually added sessions

Ever missed or mistracked a session? You could already add those manually before but they wouldn’t get the tomatoes they deserved. Not anymore! Now you'll earn tomato rewards for manually entered sessions too.

_⌨️ CTRL+OPT+R to open Review stats and activities panel, where you can add manual sessions. (See the video above for more details.)_

## ⏲️ Real-time session duration adjustments

We've all been there—deep in the zone and realizing we need just a bit more time. Now, as you adjust your session length, watch your Lifeline extend in real time. You'll see exactly when your session will end, helping you plan your day with precision.

_⌨️ CTRL+OPT+L to open the session label window during a session, then use the up and down arrows to adjust your session length. (The video has more details.)_

## 📅 Meeting length adjustment

Meeting running long? No problem. In the same way you adjust session durations, you can now also change the length of meetings on the fly.

_⌨️ CTRL+OPT+M to start a meeting; CTRL+OPT+L to open the session label window during a meeting._

These updates are all about helping you stay focused, visualize your time better, and clear that to-do list.
