Creativity Guard is a Chrome extension for mindful use of AI tools and social media. Before you open an AI assistant, it prompts you to think for yourself first: what do you actually believe the answer is?

It's the practical counterpart to the argument I keep making in [We Eat Robots](/projects/we-eat-robots): AI is most valuable when it sharpens your thinking instead of replacing it.
