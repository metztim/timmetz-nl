md-clip is a Chrome extension that turns any web page into clean Markdown via Readability extraction: one click, and the article is on your clipboard ready for your notes, your LLM context, or your static site.

Built because most of my knowledge pipeline (Logseq, Claude Code, this site) speaks Markdown, and the web doesn't.
