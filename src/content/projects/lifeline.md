Lifeline is a macOS focus timer and productivity tracker. It sits in your menu bar, tracks focused work sessions, breaks, and meetings, and shows your day as a single timeline so you can see where your time actually went.

It grew out of [Saent](/projects/saent), the productivity startup I co-founded: after the hardware years, Lifeline became the software continuation of the same mission. It holds a 4.8★ rating on the Mac App Store.

Lifeline also has an open-source companion: [lifeline-mcp](https://github.com/metztim/lifeline-mcp), an MCP server and CLI that lets Claude, ChatGPT, or any terminal read your session history and control the app remotely.
