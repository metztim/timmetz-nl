Focus and Flow for Writers

How I use AI around a draft I write myself, and the habits that keep me focused while I write it.

The Animalz AI policy says the draft is yours to write. That leaves a lot of room for AI around the draft: looking things up while you keep typing, typing up the edits you made on paper, and catching what you missed.

This site collects what works for me, Tim Metz, along with the focus habits I have relied on since long before AI.

Three first experiments

These three need a Claude account, a printer, and a timer.

  1. The next time a quote or a study is missing from your draft, leave Claude a comment and keep writing.
  2. Print your next draft and edit it with a pen, away from your desk.
  3. Set a timer for one task, and take the break when the timer ends.
